About the Yatra

A Pilgrimage Like No Other

The Adi Kailash and Om Parvat Yatra is one of the most revered spiritual pilgrimages in India. Nestled in the Vyas Valley of Uttarakhand along the Tibetan border, it offers devotees and adventurers alike a chance to witness majestic peaks steeped in ancient mythology and divine energy.

Adi Kailash (also called Chota Kailash) stands at 4,770 meters, with the sacred Jolingkong Lake and a temple dedicated to Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati at its base. Close by, Om Parvat is a natural miracle — the snow deposits itself perfectly onto dark granite ridges to spell the sacred symbol ॐ.

Travel through border towns along the gushing Kali River, experience warm Kumaoni hospitality in Gunji homestays, and undergo a transformative spiritual awakening. Not interested in a spiritual journey? Try our rugged Darma Valley Expedition or the alpine Khaliya Top Trek.

Day-by-Day Plan

Detailed Itinerary

Choose your preferred pickup point to see the full itinerary.

D1

Delhi to Pithoragarh (Drive: 460 km / 12–13 hrs)

Start early from Delhi. Drive via Haldwani and Kathgodam to reach Pithoragarh, the Mini Kashmir of Uttarakhand. Check into your hotel for dinner and rest after the long journey. Overnight stay in Pithoragarh.

D2

Pithoragarh → Dharchula → Gunji (95 km + 80 km / 7–8 hrs)

Drive to Dharchula in the morning. Complete permit clearings and border checks. Board the local 4×4 Boleros and climb through scenic gorges and waterfalls into the high-altitude Vyas Valley. Reach Gunji (3,200m). Overnight stay in homestays.

D3

Gunji → Jolingkong — Adi Kailash Darshan (35 km / 2 hrs)

Early morning drive to Jolingkong (4,570m). Breathtaking close-up view of Adi Kailash peak, Parvati Kund, and the Shiva Temple. Offer prayers, take a holy touch of the sacred water, and drive back to Gunji for overnight stay.

D4

Gunji → Nabidhang (Om Parvat) → Pithoragarh (9–10 hrs)

Start early to Nabidhang (4,250m) to witness the sacred Om Parvat. After Darshan, return to Gunji for lunch. Descend the Vyas Valley to Dharchula in 4×4 Boleros, then transfer to road vehicles and drive to Pithoragarh. Overnight stay.

D5

Pithoragarh Local Sightseeing & Rest

Explore the beautiful town of Pithoragarh — visit the historic Fort built by Chand kings, the sacred Mostamanu Temple on its scenic ridge, and Chandak Hill with panoramic valley views. Rest and prepare for the return. Overnight in Pithoragarh.

D6

Pithoragarh to Delhi (460 km / 12–13 hrs)

Drive back to Delhi through Champawat, Haldwani, and Kathgodam. Reach Delhi by late evening. Tour concludes with sacred blessings and lifelong memories.

D1

Kathgodam to Pithoragarh (185 km / 6–7 hrs)

Start from Kathgodam railway station. Drive through winding scenic hills passing Bhimtal, Champawat, and Lohaghat. Arrive in the beautiful valley of Pithoragarh. Check into your hotel for dinner and rest. Overnight in Pithoragarh.

D2

Pithoragarh → Dharchula → Gunji (95 km + 80 km / 7–8 hrs)

Drive from Pithoragarh to Dharchula in the morning. Complete permit clearings and border checks at the checkposts. Board the local 4×4 Boleros and climb through scenic rocky gorges and waterfalls into Vyas Valley. Reach Gunji (3,200m) for acclimatization. Overnight in traditional homestays.

D3

Gunji → Jolingkong — Adi Kailash Darshan (35 km / 2 hrs)

Early morning drive to Jolingkong (4,570m). Behold the breathtaking close-up view of Mount Adi Kailash. Visit the sacred Parvati Kund and the Shiva Temple at its banks. Meditate and absorb the divine energy. Drive back to Gunji for overnight stay.

D4

Gunji → Nabidhang (Om Parvat) → Pithoragarh (9–10 hrs)

Start early and drive to Nabidhang (4,250m) to witness holy Om Parvat. After Darshan and prayers at the Om Temple, return to Gunji for lunch. Board 4×4 Boleros to descend to Dharchula, then drive to Pithoragarh for a well-deserved rest. Overnight in Pithoragarh.

D5

Pithoragarh Local Sightseeing & Rest

Explore the beautiful town of Pithoragarh. Visit Pithoragarh Fort, the Mostamanu Temple on a scenic ridge, and Chandak Hill for panoramic views. Spend the evening shopping for local Kumaoni woolens and handicrafts. Overnight in Pithoragarh.

D6

Pithoragarh to Kathgodam (185 km / 6–7 hrs)

Drive back to Kathgodam through scenic routes via Champawat and Lohaghat. Drop-off at Kathgodam railway station by late evening, concluding your sacred Yatra with beautiful memories.

D1

Pithoragarh → Dharchula → Gunji (175 km / 7–8 hrs)

Start early from Pithoragarh. Reach Dharchula, complete permit clearings and border checks. Transfer to local 4×4 vehicles and climb directly to Gunji (3,200m) in Vyas Valley. Acclimatize and overnight stay in Gunji.

D2

Gunji → Adi Kailash + Om Parvat → Dharchula (140 km / 9–10 hrs)

A full day. Early morning drive to Jolingkong for Adi Kailash Darshan, then proceed to Nabidhang for Om Parvat Darshan. Return to Gunji and board 4×4 vehicles to descend to Dharchula for overnight stay.

D3

Dharchula to Pithoragarh (95 km / 4 hrs)

Relaxed morning in Dharchula. Drive back to Pithoragarh. Drop-off by afternoon, concluding your Express Yatra with sacred blessings.
