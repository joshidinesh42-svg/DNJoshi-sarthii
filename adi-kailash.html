About the Sacred Journey

The **Adi Kailash and Om Parvat Yatra** is one of the most revered and spiritually enriching pilgrimages in India. Tucked away in the Vyas Valley of Uttarakhand bordering Tibet, it offers devotees a chance to witness majestic peaks steeped in mythology.

**Adi Kailash** (often called Chota Kailash) stands at 4,770 meters. Close to its foot lies the beautiful **Jolingkong Lake** and the ancient temple dedicated to Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati. Nearby, **Om Parvat** is a mystical natural wonder where the white snow deposits itself perfectly onto the dark rock ridges to spell the sacred symbol **"ॐ"**.

During this trip, you will travel through the scenic border towns along the gushing Kali River, experience Kumaoni hospitality in local homestays in Gunji and Kutti, and undergo a truly transformative spiritual awakening. (If you wish to explore more of Uttarakhand, check out our rugged Darma Valley Expedition, or the alpine meadows of Khaliya Top Trek in Munsiyari).

Day-by-Day Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Delhi to Pithoragarh (Drive: 460 km / 12-13 hrs)

Start early morning from Delhi. Drive via Haldwani and Kathgodam to reach Pithoragarh, the Mini Kashmir of Uttarakhand. Check into your hotel for dinner and rest after the long journey. Overnight stay in Pithoragarh.

Day 2

Pithoragarh to Dharchula & Drive to Gunji/Napalchu (Drive: 95 km + 80 km / 7-8 hrs)

Drive to Dharchula in the morning. Complete permit clearings and border checks. Board the local 4x4 Boleros to climb through scenic gorges and waterfalls into the high-altitude Vyas Valley. Reach Gunji (3,200m). Overnight stay in homestays.

Day 3

Gunji to Jolingkong & Adi Kailash Darshan (Drive: 35 km / 2 hrs)

Early morning drive towards Jolingkong (4,570m). Breathtaking close-up view of Adi Kailash peak, Parvati Kund, and Shiva Temple. Offer prayers, take a holy touch of water, and drive back to Gunji for overnight stay.

Day 4

Gunji to Nabidhang (Om Parvat Darshan) & Drive to Pithoragarh (Drive: 102 km 4x4 + 95 km road / 9-10 hrs)

Start early to Nabidhang (4,250m) to witness Om Parvat. After Darshan, return to Gunji for a quick lunch. Descend down the Vyas Valley to Dharchula in 4x4 Boleros, and then transfer to your road vehicles to drive directly to Pithoragarh. Reach your hotel in Pithoragarh by late evening for a relaxing overnight stay.

Day 5

Pithoragarh Local Sightseeing & Rest

Spend the day at leisure in the beautiful town of Pithoragarh. Explore local sights like the historic Pithoragarh Fort built by Chand kings, the sacred Mostamanu Temple on a high ridge, and the serene Chandak Hill with its panoramic valley views. Rest and prepare for the return drive tomorrow. Overnight stay in Pithoragarh.

Day 6

Pithoragarh to Delhi (Drive: 460 km / 12-13 hrs)

Drive back to Delhi through Champawat, Haldwani, and Kathgodam. Reach Delhi by late evening. Tour concludes with sacred blessings and memories.

Day 1

Kathgodam to Pithoragarh (Drive: 185 km / 6-7 hrs)

Start from Kathgodam railway station/bus stand. Drive through the winding scenic hill roads passing Bhimtal, Champawat, and Lohaghat. Arrive in the beautiful valley of Pithoragarh, the Mini Kashmir of Kumaon. Check into your hotel for dinner and rest. Overnight stay in Pithoragarh.

Day 2

Pithoragarh to Dharchula & Drive to Gunji/Napalchu (Drive: 95 km + 80 km / 7-8 hrs)

Drive from Pithoragarh to Dharchula in the morning. Complete permit clearings and border checks at the checkposts. Board the local 4x4 Boleros to climb through scenic rocky gorges and waterfalls into Vyas Valley. Reach Gunji (3,200m) for acclimitization. Overnight stay in traditional homestays.

Day 3

Gunji to Jolingkong & Adi Kailash Darshan (Drive: 35 km / 2 hrs)

Early morning drive towards Jolingkong (4,570m). Behold the close-up, breathtaking view of Mount Adi Kailash. Visit the sacred Parvati Kund (take a holy dip or touch the water) and the Shiva Temple at its banks. Meditate and absorb the divine energy. Drive back to Gunji for overnight stay.

Day 4

Gunji to Nabidhang (Om Parvat Darshan) & Drive to Pithoragarh (Drive: 102 km 4x4 + 95 km road / 9-10 hrs)

Start early in the morning and drive to Nabidhang (4,250m) to witness the holy Om Parvat. After Darshan and prayers at the Om Temple, return to Gunji for a quick lunch. Post-lunch, board the local 4x4 Boleros to descend to Dharchula, then transfer to road vehicles and drive directly to Pithoragarh for a well-deserved rest. Overnight stay in Pithoragarh.

Day 5

Pithoragarh Local Sightseeing & Rest

Spend the day exploring the beautiful town of Pithoragarh. Visit the historical Pithoragarh Fort, the sacred Mostamanu Temple perched on a scenic ridge, and the serene Chandak Hill offering panoramic views of the Soar Valley. Spend the evening relaxing or shopping for local Kumaoni woolens and handicrafts. Overnight stay in Pithoragarh.

Day 6

Pithoragarh to Kathgodam (Drive: 185 km / 6-7 hrs)

Drive back to Kathgodam through scenic routes passing via Champawat and Lohaghat. Drop-off at Kathgodam railway station/bus stand by late evening, concluding your sacred Yatra with beautiful memories.

Day 1

Pithoragarh to Dharchula & Drive to Gunji (Drive: 175 km / 7-8 hrs)

Start early from Pithoragarh. Reach Dharchula, complete permit clearings and border checks. Transfer to local 4x4 vehicles and climb directly to Gunji (3,200m) in Vyas Valley. Acclimatize and overnight stay in Gunji.

Day 2

Gunji to Jolingkong (Adi Kailash) & Nabidhang (Om Parvat) & descend to Dharchula (Drive: 140 km / 9-10 hrs)

A long day. Early morning drive to Jolingkong for Adi Kailash Darshan, then proceed to Nabidhang to witness Om Parvat. Return to Gunji and board 4x4 vehicles to descend down the valley to Dharchula for overnight stay.

Day 3

Dharchula to Pithoragarh (Drive: 95 km / 4 hrs)

Relaxed morning in Dharchula. Drive back to Pithoragarh. Drop-off at Pithoragarh by afternoon, concluding your quick express Yatra.
