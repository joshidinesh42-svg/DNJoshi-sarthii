About Sarthii Travels

Our background, values, and commitment to safe spiritual yatras in the Kumaon Himalayas.

Our Philosophy

Guided by Devotion, Powered by Safety

Sarthii Travels was established in Dharchula (Pithoragarh district) to bridge the gap between pilgrims and the remote, sacred peaks of Kumaon. The name **Sarthii** (meaning "Charioteer") is inspired by Lord Krishna's role guiding Arjuna, reflecting our commitment to guide you safely through your spiritual challenges in the mountains.

We specialize in providing end-to-end logistics for the Adi Kailash & Om Parvat Yatra and the Darma Valley Expedition. These areas border Tibet and require special Inner-Line Permits, medical checks, and customized high-clearance 4x4 vehicles. We manage the entire permit paperwork, arrange vetted local homestays, and dispatch oxygen-equipped convoy vehicles.

By focusing exclusively on the Kumaon hills, we support the local border communities. All our guides, drivers, and hosts are locals who possess deep traditional knowledge of the valleys and weather patterns.

Unmatched Trust

Our tagline is **Travel With Trust**. We maintain absolute transparency in pricing, bookings, and accommodation details. No surprise fees.

Altitude Safety

Traveling above 4,500m requires medical diligence. We schedule gradual acclimatization stops, monitor oxygen levels daily, and carry high-flow oxygen cylinders.

Eco-Conscious Tourism

We encourage minimal plastic footprints and support village homestays. This directly funnels tourism revenue to remote border villagers of Kumaon.
