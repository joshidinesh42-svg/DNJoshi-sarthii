Spiritual Pilgrimage

Adi Kailash & Om Parvat Yatra

Embark on a sacred journey into the heart of Kumaon's high-altitude borderlands. The Adi Kailash peak, mirroring Mount Kailash, stands tall at 4,770 meters, alongside Om Parvat which features the natural symbol of 'Om' etched in snow. This trip covers majestic spots like Pithoragarh, Dharchula, Gunji, Jolingkong, and Nabidhang.

Max Altitude 4,770m

Scenic Valley Trek

Darma Valley Expedition

Darma Valley is a stunning Himalayan valley situated in the Pithoragarh district of Uttarakhand. Lying between the Kuthi Yankti Valley on the east and Lassar Yankti Valley on the west, it offers a fascinating glimpse into rustic Indo-Tibetan culture, panoramic views of the Panchachuli range, alpine flower valleys, and glacial streams.

Duration 6 Days
Trek Grade Moderate
Price Starts ₹22,000

Alpine Meadow Trek

Khaliya Top Trek, Munsiyari

Khaliya Top is a massive high-altitude alpine meadow (bugyal) situated at 3,500m above Munsiyari in Uttarakhand. This short but highly rewarding trek leads through dense forests of oak and rhododendron, opening up to a wide grassy ridge that faces a breathtaking close-up panoramic view of the Nanda Devi, Hardeol, Rajrambha, and Panchachuli peaks.

Duration 3 Days
Max Altitude 3,500m
Price Starts ₹14,999
